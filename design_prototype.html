The Directory is a small celebration of the people who shape a team: the makers, thinkers, organizers, and quiet problem-solvers whose work gives a company its character.

This prototype explores an editorial and botanical visual language in both a warm cream light theme and a deep midnight theme. Every interaction is intentionally lightweight, readable, and designed to keep the people in focus.
